About Sri Lanka

The Pearl of the Indian Ocean

Sri Lanka is an island just south of India, with palm-lined shores, forested mountains and tea-growing hills. A trip across the island can take you from coastal towns and fishing harbours to waterfalls and gardens in the central highlands.

History and heritage

Formerly known as Ceylon, Sri Lanka has a long history that can be seen in the ancient cities of Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa, the temples of Kandy and the streets of Galle Fort. These places tell different parts of the island's story, from its early kingdoms to its centuries of maritime trade.

Culture and everyday life

Buddhist temples, Hindu kovils, mosques and churches reflect the island's religious traditions. Sinhala and Tamil are spoken across Sri Lanka. At local markets and family-run eateries, visitors can try rice and curry, hoppers and other regional dishes.

Explore the island

Visit Jaffna for northern Tamil culture, Nuwara Eliya for tea country and Matara for the southern coast. The Destinations page brings together more places to explore.
